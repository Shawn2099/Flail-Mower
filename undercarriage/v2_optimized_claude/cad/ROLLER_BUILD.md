# V3 SIMPLE SPOOL — ROLLER (12) & IDLER (4)

Approved 2026-10-29. **No Loctite. No ring stacks. No central weld.**
Two identical drums per station; one loose spacer; one Ø25 shaft.

## Per station
- **2 identical drums** at ±62.
- **Roller drum:** 100NB pipe 114.3x4.5 x28 + backplate 6 (OD105.3/ID65, welded inside the pipe at the inner end) + bearing seat 65/52x22 (welded to the backplate; 52 H7 after welding).
- **Idler drum:** 200 OD x5 tube x40 + backplate 6 (OD190/ID65, welded inside at the inner end) + bearing seat 65/52x22.
- **1 x 6205-2RS per drum:** outer race press into 52 H7 seat; inner race press on Ø25 shaft. Bearing at the **tread centre** (12.5..27.5 from the inner face).
- **Spacer:** 32NB 42.4x3.2 x109, loose, between the two bearing inner races; no weld; passes through the backplate/seat bores.
- **Shaft:** Ø25 EN8 x260; two drums pressed at ±62; outer collars/washers/pins at the plates.
- **Outer end:** open pipe (cap optional).

## Sequence (per drum)
1. Cut pipe to length (roller 28; idler 40); deburr.
2. Weld backplate inside the pipe at the inner end; square, full fillet both sides.
3. Weld the 65/52x22 seat to the backplate; keep the seat coaxial with the pipe.
4. **Ream/face the seat bore 52 H7 after all welding.** Check roundness and squareness.
5. Press the 6205 outer race into the seat until it bottoms at the 12.5 mm depth (bearing 12.5..27.5).
6. Press the drum onto the Ø25 shaft at ±62 (inner race press fit; shaft seats 25 k5).
7. Fit the 42.4x35.5x109 spacer between the two bearing inner races; no weld.
8. Outer collars at the plates, washers + R-pins; spin test: drum free, shaft does not turn in the plates.

## Checks / reject
- Seat bore 52 H7, no weld spatter; bearing outer race fully seated.
- Bearing inner race does not spin on the shaft (25 k5 press).
- Spacer ends butt both inner races; no gap; no contact with the shaft (ID35.5 > 25).
- Drum runs true: tread runout <1.0 mm; no rock.
- Horn clearance: roller 7.5 mm (spacer bottom to horn tip); idler 49.6 mm (spacer) / 24.2 mm (pipe bore).
- Open end: cap or plug if the machine is used in wet mud.

## Mass (CAD)

- Idler drum 2.347 kg each; idler station 6.33 kg incl 2 bearings, spacer, shaft.
