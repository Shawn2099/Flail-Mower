# Track change / adjustment procedure - DRAFT (unproven until tried on the first build)

Machine: v2_optimized_claude cad_v4. Tools: spanners M12 = 19 AF, M14 = 22 AF, M16 = 24 AF, M24 = 36 AF; M8 = 13 AF; 2 jack stands; pry bars; grease; sag gauge (straight edge + ruler).
Safety: machine switched off, battery disconnected, flail rotor stopped and locked, chocks on the other track.

**Important design fact:** the roller shafts, idler shaft, spacer rods and drive axle all pass through the track loop and are held by BOTH side plates. An endless track therefore cannot be slid off sideways without taking the OUTER side plate off. Retensioning (A) is a routine job; a track change always needs (B). There is no "slack-only" method (an earlier draft had one; it cannot work).

## A. Retension (routine, ~10 min per side)
1. Park on flat ground. Measure sag at mid-span between idler and sprocket: target 15-25 mm (OEM guide range, UNVERIFIED for this track).
2. Slacken the idler shaft nuts (thin M24 nuts, 36 AF) half a turn on both sides.
3. Turn the two M16 tension rods equally (24 AF); lock the jam nuts. Travel available: 25 mm back / 35 mm forward.
4. Re-tighten the idler shaft nuts; re-check sag. Do not over-tension: drag rises and the track can pop off in a turn.

## B. Change the track (remove the outer plate), ~1.5 h per side first time
1. Jack the side under the rear cross-beam (NOT the plates, rod or idler) until the track is 20 mm off the ground. (Lift points are NOT designed yet - mark them on the first build.)
2. Wind the two M16 rods back fully and push the idler back 25 mm (slack for the later refit).
3. Remove from the OUTER plate, working outside the track: 3 roller-shaft nuts + washers (36 AF), idler shaft thin nut + washer, the tension-rod nut and lug side, the 2 spacer-rod nuts (24 AF).
4. Loosen the two grub screws of the outer UCFL208 and the chain-wheel/hub set screws. Support the outer plate, then slide the plate with the roller and idler shafts and the outer UCFL off the drive axle end together. NOTE: the outer UCFL's M14 nuts sit between the plates (track side): undo them first from outside by holding the bolt head, or leave them and take the plate off complete with its UCFL.
5. Lift the old track off the exposed shaft ends; fit the new one over the sprocket and idler; check the horns sit in the sprocket gorge (24 mm) and the idler/roller groove (70 mm).
6. Refit the outer plate in reverse order. Torque draft values: M24 shaft nuts about 100 N.m, M14 UCFL bolts 80 N.m, M12 beam bolts 80 N.m, M16 rods hand-tight plus jam nut. Retension as in A.

## Known gaps
- Lift points / lift lugs are not designed. - Access to the UCFL nuts between the plates needs a trial on the first article.
- Idea for a faster service later: make the outer plate removable as one bolted unit (shafts captured in it), so step B takes 6 nuts.
