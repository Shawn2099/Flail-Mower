Simple spool proposal - roller & idler

Pipe + backplate with welded bearing seat + loose spacer. No Loctite. No ring stacks. Two identical drums per station. Units: mm.

Please inspect: bearing is at the tread centre in a welded 65/52 seat; the spacer is loose between the two bearing inner races; the outer pipe end is open (cap optional); the shaft is a press fit in both bearings. This is a proposal, not frozen.
Roller Ø114.3 × 40 - section through one station
Idler Ø200 × 40 - section through one station
