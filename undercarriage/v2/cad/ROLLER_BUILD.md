# ROLLER DRUM BUILD — 12 nos (3 stations x paired discs x 2 sides). MS-ERW, no lathe.

## Per-drum parts (BOM)
- Pipe 100NB-M 114.3x4.5, cut 28 long (12) - plus 2x6 cheeks = 40 overall
- Cheek OD114/ID65/6mm laser (24) — STRUCTURAL ring stiffeners, not covers
- Hub: 5x laser rings OD65/ID52x8mm stacked to 40 (60 for rollers + 20 idlers = 80) + 6205-2RS (16)
- Shaft dia25 bright (shared 1/station), collars + M6 grubs + R-pins + washers

## Sequence (per drum)
1. Cut pipe 28mm, deburr ID/OD, grind weld zones bright (both ends, 10mm band).
2. Trial-fit: cheek OD flush with pipe OD; hub slides through cheek ID65 by hand (0.05-0.15 gap for Loctite).
3. HUB STACK (no lathe — the 6205 itself is the jig):
   a. Laser rings OD65/ID52x8, deburr + acetone degrease (oil/scale kills Loctite).
   b. Mask both bearing seals with tape (glue in seals = dead bearing).
   c. Thin 638 film on ring faces, stack 5 on the bearing OD, clamp between 2 flats (G-clamp, light).
   d. Wipe bore squeeze-out, cure 24hr. Result: 40mm hub with bearing seated.
   e. Check: stack stands square (no rock), inner race spins free by hand.
4. Loctite 638: hub OD into cheek ID65 (0.05-0.15 gap), square, cure 24hr before load.
5. Weld cheeks to pipe ends: 3mm fillet OUTSIDE only. Stitch 4x25mm opposite sides first (distortion), then continuous.
6. Grind belt-side tread flush — no weld bead above OD114 (belt runs here). Dye-check: no porosity on tread.
7. Slip drums onto shaft at track+-62 (2/station). Bearing bore + Loctite 638 on shaft.
8. Shaft through plate D26 holes; collars butt both plate faces; M6 grubs + Loctite; washer + R-pin outboard.
9. Spin test: drum spins free, shaft does NOT turn in plates. Runout <1mm on tread.
10. Paint red-oxide + enamel (bore masked). 2RS = sealed, no greasing.

## Reject rules
- Cheek gap >0.25 (Loctite won't fill) | weld bead on tread | drum binds on shaft | shaft spins in plates.
